Projects

My projects have involved machine learning, gravitational waves, and radio astronomy to study the universe. I've developed a radio telescope to map the Milky Way's 21 cm hydrogen line (1420 MHz) and coded machine learning programs to predict exoplanet habitability and classify gravitational waves.

Astrophotgraphy

View a gallery of astrophotos I've taken that document my hobby's evolution over the years. Spanning from nebulae millions of light-years away to the Moon just in our cosmic backyard, my captures continue to explore what's beyond our crust.
